How to play a build

Three steps from download to the Battle Bus.

1

Download a build

Install a free torrent app like qBittorrent, then hit Torrent on the build you want. It opens straight in the app.

2

Extract it

Unzip the download with 7-Zip or WinRAR into a folder with a short path, like C:\Builds\12.50.

3

Launch it

Open the Tilted launcher, point it at the extracted folder and start the game.

FAQ

Which build should I pick?

Every season has one build here, picked as the best version of that season. Just choose the season you want to play.

Why are builds downloaded as torrents?

Builds are huge, and hosting them on a server costs money, which is why other build sites are full of ads. With torrents, everyone downloading also shares the pieces they already have, so the site stays free and ad-free. Please leave the torrent seeding for a while after it finishes to help the next person.

Why is a download button greyed out?

That build is listed but its link hasn't been added yet. Check back later.

How much space do I need?

Older Chapter 1 builds are smaller; Chapter 2 builds can be much bigger. Leave room for both the download and the extracted folder.
